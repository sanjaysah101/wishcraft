# WishCraft privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

WishCraft is an app that runs inside [Anna](https://anna.partners). This page explains what happens to the information you give it.

## What you type

To write a card, WishCraft uses the details you enter: the occasion, the recipient's name and relationship, the things you write about them, your name, and the language and tone you choose.

These details go only to Anna's own AI services through Anna. They are used to write the message, paint the card art, record the voice note and compose the song. WishCraft has no servers of its own, and it never sends your details to any other service.

## What is saved

Each card you create is saved to **your own Anna storage** so you can find it again in **My cards** on any device. That includes the card's words, the finished card image, the art, the voice note and the song. WishCraft also remembers your last choices, such as your name, language, tone, art style and extras, so the form is ready next time. It also keeps the form you are filling in and which card is open, so if you close WishCraft halfway through, it picks up where you left off.

Only you can see this data. The developer of WishCraft cannot read it.

## Deleting your data

- To delete a card, open **My cards**, choose **Delete** under the card, then **Delete card**. The card and its files are removed from your Anna storage.
- If you uninstall WishCraft, Anna handles the rest of the app's stored data under [Anna's own policies](https://anna.partners).

## Credits

Creating cards uses Anna credits from your account. WishCraft shows an estimate before you create anything. WishCraft never charges you directly, and it cannot see your payment details.

## Children

WishCraft is meant for anyone who can use Anna under Anna's terms. Don't put sensitive personal information, such as addresses, ID numbers or health records, on a card.
